description: Direction, the faculty between design and the threads that do the work.
dependencies: []
---

Work on a subject goes to the running thread that holds that subject. Only a small thing that no thread holds goes to a fiber.

When a line lands in spirit, intent or vision, the design thread that wrote it gets one line from direction saying so.

A fiber is kept through the thread's session and used again for the same kind of work.
