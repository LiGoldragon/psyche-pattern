---
description: Coordination, the faculty that stands between direction and the other threads.
dependencies: []
---

Direction sets what is wanted, in general. Coordination applies it: the messages of the other threads go to coordination by default and are answered there, and only what coordination cannot answer reaches direction.

What direction has no clear place for goes to coordination, which finds the thread it belongs to. Where no thread fits, a new thread is started for it, by agreement between coordination and direction.

Coordination holds no whole work. A work goes to the thread that implements its subject.
